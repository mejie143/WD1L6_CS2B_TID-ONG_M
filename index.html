MY KINGKY IDENTITY

Design Philosophy

"To be understandable and meaningful, BE SIMPLE."

My design philosophy focuses on simplicity, elegance and functionality. I believe that a good website exudes these and further attracts people who are simple minded.

It is preferable actually to have clean layouts, readable fonts, balanced spacing and colors that complement each other. Also, I believe every element in a website should have purpose.

Moreover, my style depends on my mood and likeness, and especially how I perceive my own delight in designs to how I want others to view it as appealing and useful.

1. Simplicity

Be simple. Be neat. Beat it

2. Functionality

Work it. Slay it. Do big

3. Elegance

Exude your elegance all over, gurll
